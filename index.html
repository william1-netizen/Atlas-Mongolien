The detailed 3D models didn't load, so the game is using simple shapes.
  • Flight school: 16 lessons built from your course book, from the walk-around inspection to the check ride, plus ground-school questions, checklists, limits and a logbook.
  • Time runs at real speed, like a real flight. The time button under the wind stack fast-forwards 2× or 4×. The instructor gives tips as you fly, with the book section to read.
  • Every flight starts on the ground: lay out, cold inflation with the fan, hot inflation (hold BURN to stand the balloon up), then 16 passengers board around the pilot.
  • Hold BURN to heat the envelope and climb, VENT to sink. The balloon answers slowly: burn early, stop early.
  • You can't steer. The colored streaks are the air: mint low, pink middle, gold high, bright end pointing where it goes. Pick your height to pick your direction.
  • BURN fires two of the four burners; hold it over 2 s and all four fire for a strong climb. VENT opens the parachute: tap it, never hold more than 3 s. Stay under 800 ft/min up or down and 120 °C in the envelope. Tap Propane to switch between the 4 cylinders.
  • To land: call LANDING POSITION, turn the pilot lights off, touch down under 400 ft/min with the basket's long side forward (use the turning vents ⟲ ⟳), then pull the RED LINE on the ground.
  • The control lines hang into the basket like on the real thing: red/white = parachute (VENT), black = rotate left, green = rotate right, red = rapid deflation, ground only. The white crown line is for the crown crew during inflation.
  • Every time a balloon lands, Chakib and his brother Zaml drive out from their café in their old blue Fiat Uno with coffee for everyone. The black Fortuner chase car and the drone pilot's Logan go to the landing too.
  • Emergency drills from the book: pilot lights going out, or a fire at a cylinder. Follow the steps on the red card.
Drag to look, pinch or scroll to zoom. Keys: Space burn, ↓ vent, Q / E spin, L landing position, F pilot lights, R red line, C camera.
