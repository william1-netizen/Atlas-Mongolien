# Atlas Dawn Flight

A hot-air balloon simulator and flight school over Marrakech (Bourous launch field).

Play it at the GitHub Pages address of this repository. On an iPhone: open it in Safari on Wi-Fi, wait for "✓ Saved on this phone", then Share → Add to Home Screen. After that it works offline.

The flight school is a study aid written from a balloon-pilot course book. Your national rules and your balloon's flight manual come first, and a simulator doesn't replace flying with an instructor.
